## Why Zylto?

Built and maintained by [Muzzamil Shahzad](https://www.linkedin.com/in/shahzamil/), Zylto avoids the generic scores and paywalls common to many resume checkers. Every score is backed by research into how each platform parses, filters, and ranks candidates. No paywalls, no premium tiers, just practical feedback so you can understand how your resume may be evaluated.
